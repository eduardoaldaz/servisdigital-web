# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + Tailwind CSS 4, the same stack as DocusAI so the family shares fonts, wordmark pattern and components. Blog in MDX files inside the repo (`src/content/blog`). Chosen by the user.

## Users

- **Owners and managers of SMEs and mid-sized companies in Ecuador and Spain** (any sector) who lose hours to manual work: typing documents, chasing approvals by WhatsApp, answering the same questions, reconciling spreadsheets. They are not technical; they want to know what AI can do in *their* business, how much effort it takes and who will do it.
- Secondary: finance/operations leads and accountants who will use the result, and existing FacServis or DocusAI customers looking for custom work.

## Product Purpose

servisdigital.com is the parent brand site. Servis Digital is a company that **implements AI inside companies and their processes**: it maps how work is done today, redesigns the process with AI where it pays off, and builds the custom software that makes it real (including integrations with ERPs and e-invoicing). Success = a visitor books a 30-minute meeting.

## Positioning

A current, AI-first company that builds and runs its own AI software products (FacServis in Ecuador, DocusAI in Spain). It does not only advise on AI: it ships and operates it. Process first, then AI, then software; for every sector and niche.

## Operating Context

- Markets: Ecuador and Spain. Spanish, neutral register (usted/tú neutral, avoid "vosotros").
- Products in the family: **FacServis** (facservis.com, electronic invoicing SRI + inventory, purchases, receivables; teal-black + amber) and **DocusAI** (docusai.servisdigital.com, AI capture of supplier invoices for Spanish gestorías/SMEs; violet). DocusAI is pre-launch.
- Family grammar shared with FacServis/DocusAI: Figtree + Bricolage Grotesque, two-tone wordmark, mono eyebrows, alternating dark/light sections.
- Primary action: book a meeting (Cal.com/Calendly via `NEXT_PUBLIC_BOOKING_URL`), with a contact form as fallback and WhatsApp as alternative.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: AI implementation (document reading, assistants/agents, AI over company data, automation), process discovery and digitalisation roadmap, custom software (web, mobile, internal systems), integrations and ERP (Business Central, e-invoicing SRI/Spain, APIs, data migration).
- Data protection: RGPD (Spain) and LOPDP (Ecuador).

## Brand Commitments

- Name **Servis Digital**, domain servisdigital.com. Parent of FacServis and DocusAI; the three must look like one family, each with its own color.
- Voice: direct, concrete, no hype, same tone as facservis.com ("No inventamos testimonios").

## Evidence on Hand

The user confirmed real evidence exists but has not provided it yet: FacServis customers (FacServis already shows three named testimonials with RUC), delivered custom projects, real figures. Until provided, **nothing is invented**: case studies and figures live in `src/content/pruebas.ts` and render only when filled (a dashed placeholder shows in development).

## Product Principles

1. Process before technology: show the process changing, not abstract AI.
2. Prove with our own products and concrete examples, never with invented numbers.
3. Every page leads to one action: book a meeting.
4. The parent brand frames the products; each product keeps its own color.
